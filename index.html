MathHacks is a learning-focused hackathon designed for students interested in computer science. We'll be hosting an in-person event with exciting workshops, keynotes, mini-events, and much more!

MathHacks 2.0 runs October 17–18, 2026 at room 204 of the Bergeron Centre, Lassonde School of Engineering, York University (North York, Toronto) — 8:00 AM Saturday through 8:00 PM Sunday.

No, you do not need any knowledge about programming to participate! MathHacks is designed to be beginner-friendly. You only need to be ready to learn!

Bring your laptop, charger, and readiness to learn! We'll provide all food and drink for the entire event.
